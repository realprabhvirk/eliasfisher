About Elias

Close to 30 years of hands-on massage work

The last 10 of them spent running this as a dedicated, full-time Sports & Remedial Massage practice.

His approach

Continuously refining his skills, focused on the client

Elias describes his approach simply: he is focused on the client and the result, not on himself. That means treatment built around what the person on the table actually needs, drawn from close to three decades of hands-on experience, and continuously refined rather than run off a fixed script.

He holds an Advanced Diploma in Health Science and a Diploma in Remedial Massage, and works across Remedial Massage, Deep Tissue, Dry Needling, Trigger Point Therapy, Cupping, Myofascial Therapy, Lymphatic Drainage, Swedish massage and Muscle Energy Techniques (MET).

Where he works now

A new studio, purpose set up for this work

Elias recently moved into a new studio space at 7/11 Nullum Street, Murwillumbah. It is the physical setting for the same approach he has always used: quiet, focused, one-on-one treatment time with no distractions.

His own clients have noticed the change. Rachel Trevaskis, a client, said Elias "is very professional and has a great new location and workspace."
